Lucent Chess — Third-Party Visual Assets

Except for Lucent's locally generated Fritz-inspired set, the bundled chess
piece sets and board themes come from the official Lichess
source repository:
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/images/board

Authors and per-set licenses are documented in the accompanying
LICHESS-COPYING.md. The Lichess AGPLv3 license is included as
LICHESS-AGPL-3.0.txt, and GPLv2 as GPL-2.0.txt.

The reillycraig and riohacha piece directories are not bundled because the
upstream license table does not state redistribution terms for them.

All included upstream assets are unmodified.
